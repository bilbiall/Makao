03

Payments & your data — the two questions every landlord actually asks

Before "does it have a nice dashboard," every landlord asks two things: does it touch my money, and who can see my tenants. Straight answers to both.

Y

Every shilling lands in your account — not ours

STK Push and C2B both run on your own M-Pesa Paybill or Till, using Daraja API credentials you set once per property. Renty is never in the money's path — we don't hold it, move it, or take a cut before it reaches you. We just watch the same API Safaricom already gives you.

A

Payments record themselves

The moment a tenant pays, Safaricom's callback reaches Renty in real time. The phone number or account reference on that payment is matched automatically to the right tenant and invoice — balance updated, receipt issued — with no landlord ever opening a bank statement to reconcile by hand.

D

Your data stays yours

Every landlord's properties, tenants and payment history live in their own isolated slice of the database, enforced at the query level, not just hidden in a menu. Grant a staff member exactly the access they need — no more — with custom staff roles, and pull any tenant, invoice or payment list to PDF whenever you want a copy outside Renty.

04

Why this, not a generic property tool

Global property-management software treats Kenya as an afterthought. Renty was built the other way around.

M

M-Pesa is the foundation, not a plugin

STK Push and C2B Paybill reconciliation are core to the product — a payment matches a tenant automatically, by account number or phone, with no manual bank-statement reading.

2

Long-term and short-stay, one system

A landlord running both an apartment block and a BnB unit doesn't need two subscriptions and two logins — it's one portfolio, one dashboard.

S

Works over SMS, not just data

Bills, reminders and announcements reach every tenant's phone — feature phone or smartphone, data bundle or none.

R

Delegate without losing control

Custom staff roles let a growing landlord hand off day-to-day work — admitting tenants, recording payments, sending announcements — permission by permission, not all-or-nothing.

T

Tenants help themselves

An SMS invite code lets a new tenant verify and join their own tenancy — no password to hand over, no support call to make it happen.

K

Built by, and for, this market

Not a Silicon Valley template with M-Pesa added later — the payment reconciliation, the SMS-first communication, and the pricing are Kenyan from day one.
